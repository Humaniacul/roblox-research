# roblox-research

This repo holds research into the next step for the Roblox "Steal an X" genre (Steal a Brainrot, Steal an Egg and their variants). The focus is on new gameplay mechanics, not new collectibles.

- **[REPORT.md](REPORT.md):** the full report (v1, 2026-10-15). It covers:
  - **Part A:** market intelligence, including the genre timeline, the variant ecosystem grouped by acquisition verb, the psychology behind the formula, and the 2026 platform changes.
  - **Part B:** an opportunity map.
  - **Part C:** eight concepts that survived screening, plus a list of the eliminated ones.
  - **Part D:** a scored ranking.
  - **Part E:** deep dives on the top 3.
  - **Part F:** the final recommendation and what to prototype first.
  - Research limitations, the pending competition sweep, and sources.

**Status:** competition checks specific to each concept are still pending. Part C lists the queries for the next research pass.
